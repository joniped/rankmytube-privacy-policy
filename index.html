1. Information We Collect

A. Personal & Account Data

When you sign in using Google Sign-In (Firebase Authentication), we collect and store:

  • Your Display Name and Email Address
  • Your Google Profile Picture URL
  • Your Unique User Identifier (Firebase UID)

B. Device, Network & Diagnostic Data

To ensure app stability, prevent abuse, and deliver updates, we automatically collect:

  • Device Details: Device brand, model, OS version, and app-scoped Device ID.
  • Network & Region: IP address and country-level location.
  • Push Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to deliver in-app notifications.
  • Crash Logs: Stack traces and diagnostic reports if the app experiences an error.

C. SEO & AI Tool Inputs

When you use RankMyTube features (AI Coach, Keyword Research, Title/Description/Tag Generators, Video SEO Audit, Thumbnail Analyzer, Script Writer, and Content Extractor), we process the keywords, prompts, and public YouTube URLs you submit to generate your results.

D. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Pro subscriptions (rankmytube_weekly, rankmytube_monthly, rankmytube_yearly) are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your credit card or banking details; we only store your subscription tier (free or pro) and expiry date.

  • To authenticate your account and synchronize your user profile.
  • To provide AI-powered YouTube SEO recommendations, channel analytics, and tag extraction.
  • To verify Pro subscription status and disable advertisements for subscribed users.
  • To diagnose crashes, respond to user feedback, and broadcast important app updates.

We retain your account information only while your account is active. You may permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time:

Method 1 (Instant In-App Deletion): Open RankMyTube → Navigate to Profile → Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Method 2 (Email Request): Email entatena1@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Delete RankMyTube Account". We process all deletion requests within 7 business days.

All data transmitted between RankMyTube and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Users in the EEA, UK, California, and other jurisdictions have the right to access, export, or erase their personal data, and may opt out of personalized AdMob advertisements via their Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
